The whole county

One Roofing Contractor for All of Delaware County

Delaware County covers 457 square miles, and the roofs change as you drive across it. The southern townships filled with subdivisions during the 1990s and 2000s. The City of Delaware has a downtown historic district and a National Register neighborhood of more than 500 homes and carriage houses. The north and west still have small villages laid out in the 1800s surrounded by farmland. Our Delaware County roofing overview covers what ties the county together; the pages below cover each community.

Every community page covers the same three things: the housing stock and what it means for your roof, the verified storms that have hit nearby, and the permit office and HOA or historic review that apply there. Wherever you live, inspections are free and quotes come in writing with the permit included. If you are planning a replacement, start with our Delaware County roof replacement cost guide.

On the job

In our experience, the most common mix-up we sort out for new customers is the mailing address. Olentangy Falls homes carry a Delaware mailing address but sit in Liberty Township, and plenty of Powell addresses are outside Powell's city limits. It matters because it decides which building department issues the permit and which rules apply. We look up the parcel before we quote, so homeowners do not have to figure that out themselves.

South county

Southern Delaware County

The fastest growth in Ohio's fastest-growing county happened here, and much of it in a short span — which is why so many roofs are coming due together. Our builder-grade roof lifespan guide explains the warning signs to watch for on boom-era homes, and several south-county neighborhoods have design boards that want to see your shingle color first.

  • Powell — a city that grew from 6,247 residents in 2000 to 14,163 in 2020, including Golf Village. Powell requires a photo-documented permit for every re-roof.
  • Lewis Center and Orange Township — the township's housing permits peaked at 762 in 2004; Evans Farm adds new design-reviewed homes.
  • Liberty Township — one-acre farm-residence lots, Olentangy Falls and the area around the Columbus Zoo.
  • Genoa Township — 3,810 homes permitted from 1998 to 2003 on the Delaware County side of Westerville, plus Hoover Reservoir.
  • Concord Township — Scioto Reserve (1998) and Tartan Fields (1997), with HOA review of shingle colors.

Central county

Delaware and the Center of the County

These communities have seen the most tornado activity in recent years: EF0s near Delaware Lake in 2016 and east of the city in 2020, then the 2024 EF1. Our storm damage checklist is written for them. Homes inside downtown Delaware's historic district also need a Certificate of Appropriateness for roof work, which we file before the building permit.

  • City of Delaware — the county seat, home to Ohio Wesleyan, the Downtown Historic District (Certificate of Appropriateness required for roofs) and the Historic Northwest Neighborhood.
  • Berlin Township — newer HOA subdivisions on former farmland, and the heart of the March 2024 EF1 tornado path.

East county

Sunbury, Galena and the East Side

Both sit along the path of the March 2024 tornado, which the National Weather Service tracked past Sunbury and Galena with peak winds of 110 mph. If your roof was patched after that storm, a follow-up roof inspection is worth scheduling.

  • Sunbury — a city since 2021 with an 1816 town square, the Northstar community and the I-71/US-36 corridor.
  • Galena — a village platted in 1816, listed among the jurisdictions hit by the 2024 tornado.

North & west

Northern and Western Villages

Older homes in these villages often call for a repair-first approach, and we offer metal roofing for homes and outbuildings where it fits. Call (740) 555-0177 to schedule a free inspection anywhere in the county.

  • Ashley — a village laid out in 1849 in Oxford Township, in the rural north of the county.
  • Ostrander — a railroad village from 1852 in Scioto Township that grew about 70 percent from 2010 to 2020.

Frequently asked

Do you serve homes with a Westerville or Columbus address in Delaware County?

Yes. Parts of Genoa and Orange townships have been annexed into Westerville and Columbus. We serve those homes and pull the permit from the city that has jurisdiction — see our roof permit guide.

Do you charge more to travel to Ashley or Ostrander?

No. Inspections are free anywhere in Delaware County, and our pricing is based on the roof, not the distance.

My town isn't listed. Do you still cover it?

Very likely. We serve all of Delaware County, including smaller places like Shawnee Hills and the rural townships. Call (740) 555-0177 and ask.

Which part of the county gets the most storm damage?

Storms don't follow township lines, but recent tornadoes have tracked through the center and east of the county, including Berlin Township, Sunbury and Galena in 2024. Any home can take wind or hail damage; see our storm damage roof repair page.
