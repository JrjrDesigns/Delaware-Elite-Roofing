Who we are

A Roofing Contractor Focused on Delaware County

Delaware Elite Roofing serves homeowners across Delaware County, Ohio — the subdivisions of Powell, Lewis Center and the southern townships, the historic streets of the City of Delaware, and the villages to the north and west. We focus on this county on purpose. Its roofs have specific needs: thousands of builder-grade roofs from the 1998–2008 building boom reaching the end of their life, HOA design boards that review shingle colors, several different permit offices, and a real tornado and windstorm history. Knowing those details is most of the job.

We are licensed and insured, and we handle residential roof replacement, roof repair, storm damage claims documentation, gutters and 24/7 emergency tarping.

What we will not do matters as much. We do not quote a price before we have seen the roof. We do not pressure anyone to sign on the spot, and we do not recommend a full replacement when a repair will honestly do. When a roof needs to come off, we install it to the Residential Code of Ohio — full tear-off when two layers already exist, an ice barrier at the eaves and proper ventilation — and we pick shingle lines that hold up to the wind on open subdivision lots. If your HOA reviews colors, our HOA-approved roof replacement service takes care of the application.

Our process

How We Work

Every job follows the same steps, whether it is a single pipe boot or a full tear-off:

  • Free inspection. We look at every slope, the flashing, vents, gutters and attic, and photograph what we find. You see what we see — not a verbal summary from the ladder.
  • Straight recommendation. Repair, replace or leave it alone. If the roof has years left, we tell you so.
  • Written, line-item scope. Shingle line and color, layers removed, underlayment and ice barrier, ventilation, flashing, decking price per sheet, permit, cleanup and warranty — all on paper.
  • Approvals first. We file HOA design-review applications and confirm whether your permit comes from Delaware County Building Safety, the City of Delaware, Powell or Westerville. See our roof permit guide.
  • Clean build. Tarps over landscaping, magnetic nail sweeps of the yard and driveway, and photos of each stage — which Powell's permit office requires anyway.
  • Final walk-through. We walk the job with you and hand over the warranty paperwork.

Insurance

How We Handle Storm Claims

When a storm is involved, we document damage with dated photos and measurements and meet your adjuster on site. We do not ask for power of attorney, negotiate your claim for you or offer to absorb your deductible — the Ohio Department of Insurance advises homeowners against those arrangements, and we agree. Our Ohio roof insurance claims guide explains your rights and the deadlines insurers must meet.

On the job

We hear from a lot of Delaware County homeowners who have had three or four roofers knock on the door after a storm, each with a different story about what the roof needs. Most of them just want someone to show them the evidence. So that is how we run an inspection: we hand over the photos, point to the specific shingles, and let the homeowner decide. People are far more comfortable making a big decision when they have seen their own roof up close.

Frequently asked

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Ohio does not have a statewide residential roofing license, so ask any roofer for proof of liability insurance and workers' compensation. We provide ours with every estimate.

Do you use subcontractors?

Ask us on your inspection and we'll tell you exactly who will be on your roof and who supervises the job. You'll have one point of contact from estimate to final walk-through.

What warranty do you offer?

Every quote states the manufacturer's material warranty for the shingle line and our workmanship warranty in writing, so you can compare it directly with other bids.

Do you only do roof replacements?

No. A lot of our work is repairs, leak tracing and inspections. If a roof leak repair solves the problem, that's what we'll recommend. Call (740) 555-0177 to set up a visit.
