What happens next

What to Expect When You Call

We will ask for your address, the age of the roof if you know it, and what you are seeing — a leak, missing shingles, storm damage or just a roof that is getting old. We then schedule an inspection, look at every slope, the flashing, vents and attic, and give you photos and a written recommendation. If you need a quote, it comes as a line-item roof replacement or roof repair scope with the permit included.

If water is coming in after a storm, call (740) 555-0177 any time for emergency roof tarping. Helpful reading before your visit: our storm damage checklist and roofing FAQ.

If you live in an HOA neighborhood, mention it when you reach out. Approval from a design board can take time, and we like to start the application as soon as you have chosen a shingle color so it does not hold up your schedule.

Frequently asked

How soon can you inspect my roof?

Usually within a few business days, and sooner for active leaks. After a major storm, emergency calls are triaged first, so call as early as you can.

Do I need to be home for the inspection?

Not for the exterior, though we recommend it so we can show you the photos and check the attic. If your insurance adjuster is coming, we'll try to schedule at the same time.

Do you work on Saturdays?

Our office is open Saturdays 8 a.m. to 2 p.m., and storm and leak calls are answered 24/7.

What should I have ready when I call?

Your address, the approximate roof age, your HOA name if you have one, and photos if you've taken them. If a storm is involved, note the date — our roof insurance claims guide explains why.
