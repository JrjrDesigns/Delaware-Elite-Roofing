Why gutters matter

What your gutters are actually protecting

A gutter's job is simple: collect the water that comes off the roof and put it somewhere harmless. When gutters overflow, pull away from the fascia, or dump downspout water right next to the foundation, that water goes where it does damage – into fascia boards and soffits, behind siding, into window wells and basements, and across walkways where it freezes in winter.

That matters more in Delaware County than many homeowners realize. The subdivisions that went up across Genoa Township, Orange, Liberty and Concord townships during the 1990s and 2000s are full of large two-story homes with big, steep roof planes, and many of their gutter systems are original. After 20-plus years, seams separate, hangers loosen, pitch sags and downspouts get crushed by mowers and snowplows.

We install seamless aluminum gutters formed on site to fit each run, with hidden hangers, properly sized downspouts and outlets placed where the water actually concentrates. Call (740) 555-0177 for a free gutter and roof-edge assessment.

Local conditions

Gutters and Delaware County weather

Central Ohio's spring thunderstorms dump a lot of water fast, and its winters hover around freezing for weeks. Both are hard on gutters. Summer cloudbursts expose undersized systems, and winter freeze-and-thaw turns clogged gutters into ice-filled troughs that pull away from the fascia under the weight.

Gutters and ice dams are tied together. When attic heat melts snow on the upper roof and it refreezes at the eaves, ice builds up in gutters and along the roof edge, and water backs up under the shingles. Gutters do not cause ice dams, but clogged, sagging gutters make them worse. The Residential Code of Ohio's requirement for an ice barrier from the eave edge to at least 24 inches inside the exterior wall line is the roof-side protection; free-draining gutters are the other half. If you have had winter leaks at the eaves, our roof leak repair team can look at both.

Wind is the other factor. In the open subdivisions of Berlin Township and Liberty Township, where former farm fields offer little shelter, wind can catch a loose gutter run and pull it free. The March 14, 2024 EF1 tornado that crossed the county tore at roof edges along its path. If your gutters were bent or loosened in that storm, they may be part of an insurance claim – our storm damage roof repair service documents gutter and downspout damage alongside the roof.

Trees

Young trees, old trees and gutter guards

Much of southern Delaware County has a young tree canopy – the subdivisions are only 15 to 30 years old, and many were built on open farmland. Homes there often need less gutter cleaning than older neighborhoods, and guards are frequently unnecessary. As those street trees mature, that changes, and seed pods and small leaves start to show up in the gutters every year.

The older parts of the county are a different story. Homes in the historic cores of the City of Delaware, Sunbury and Galena often sit under large, mature trees that fill gutters every fall. For those homes, a quality guard can be worth it. We will tell you honestly which camp your house falls into.

On the job

In our experience with Evans Farm-style homes built with bungalow and craftsman porches tucked under a taller main roof, the trouble spot is a second-story downspout that empties onto the porch roof below. Every storm, the full flow from the upper roof hits one small patch of porch shingles, wearing them out years ahead of the rest of the roof and overwhelming the porch gutter. Extending that downspout down to the lower gutter, or rerouting it, protects both.

Assessment

What we check on a gutter visit

A gutter assessment is more than looking for clogs. We walk the perimeter and check the pitch of every run, look for daylight at seams and corners, test hangers for looseness, and note where fascia paint is peeling or wood is soft – the telltale sign of years of overflow. We look at where each downspout discharges and whether the ground there slopes away from the house or back toward it. On the roof edge, we check whether drip edge is present and lapped into the gutter, because water that runs behind the gutter rots the fascia even when the gutter itself is fine.

Whether your home is a two-story in Powell, a newer build in Lewis Center or an older house near a village square, if it has a basement, a downspout that dumps beside a window well is worth fixing before it becomes a basement water problem. We will note anything like that, even when it is outside the scope of the gutter job itself.

Timing

Replace gutters with your roof, or separately?

If your roof and gutters are the same age, replacing them together usually makes sense. During a roof replacement, new drip edge goes on, and new gutters can be hung to fit it exactly without disturbing a brand-new roof later. If your roof is newer than your gutters, a gutter-only job is fine. Either way, a roof inspection includes a look at the gutters, fascia and downspouts so you know where things stand. Frequently asked

My gutters are as old as my subdivision roof. Do they go at the same time?

If they are the same age or in poor shape, yes. New drip edge and new gutters fit together correctly, and you avoid disturbing a new roof edge later to swap gutters. If your gutters are newer and sound, we can protect and reuse them.

Why do my gutters overflow at one corner?

Usually because a roof valley concentrates water into an inside corner faster than the gutter and nearest downspout can carry it away. An extra downspout, a larger gutter on that run, or a diverter typically fixes it.

Do I need gutter guards in a newer Delaware County subdivision?

Often not yet. Many subdivisions in the southern townships have young street trees that drop little debris. Homes under mature trees in Delaware, Sunbury or Galena are better candidates. We will give you an honest recommendation.

Can sagging gutters cause ice dams?

Sagging or clogged gutters do not cause ice dams on their own, but they hold water and ice at the eave and make dams worse. Attic heat loss, ventilation and the roof's ice barrier are the main factors.

Were my gutters damaged in the 2024 tornado?

If you are near the March 14, 2024 path through Berlin Township, Sunbury or Galena, check for dents, bent hangers and gutters pulled away from the fascia. Gutter damage can be part of a storm claim. Call (740) 555-0177 for an inspection.

How far should downspouts discharge from my foundation?

Far enough that water flows away from the house rather than pooling near the foundation – extensions or splash blocks that carry water several feet out and onto ground that slopes away are common. We set extensions as part of every install.
