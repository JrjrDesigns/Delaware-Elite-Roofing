Frequently asked

How many days will a roofing crew be at my house?

Most single-family re-roofs are installed in one to two days once materials and approvals are in place. Weather, roof size, steep pitch and decking repairs can add time. HOA review and permits happen before that, so plan on more lead time overall. See our roof replacement page for the full process.

What is the difference between 3-tab and architectural shingles?

Three-tab shingles are a single flat layer with a uniform look. Architectural, or dimensional, shingles are laminated in layers, look thicker and generally resist wind better. Architectural shingles cost a little more — see the price ranges in our cost guide.

Can a new roof go over my old shingles?

Only if there is just one existing layer. The Residential Code of Ohio does not allow a new covering over a roof that already has two or more layers. We generally recommend a full tear-off anyway so the decking can be inspected.

Does Ohio require an ice barrier on a new roof?

Yes. Ohio's residential code requires an ice barrier from the eave edge to at least 24 inches inside the exterior wall line. It protects against water backing up under shingles when snow and ice sit at the eaves.

Does Ohio license roofing contractors?

Ohio has no statewide residential roofing license. Some cities have their own contractor registration, and the City of Delaware stopped requiring contractor registration in 2026. Ask any roofer for proof of liability insurance and workers' compensation, and read how we work.

What should I do if my roof is leaking right now?

Move belongings, catch the water and, if a ceiling is bulging, relieve it into a bucket. Then call (740) 555-0177 — we take leak and storm calls 24/7 and can tarp the roof. Our emergency roof tarping page explains what happens next.

Why does my roof leak only in heavy rain or wind-driven rain?

That pattern usually points to flashing — around chimneys, walls, skylights or pipe boots — rather than the shingles themselves. Wind pushes water sideways into gaps that stay dry in a gentle rain. Our roof leak repair service traces the source before we fix it.

How do I know if hail damaged my roof?

Look for dents in gutters, downspouts and the AC condenser, granules piling up at downspouts and dark spots on shingles. Hail damage is hard to confirm from the ground, so a free roof inspection is the reliable way to know.

Is a re-roof a good time to deal with sagging or leaking gutters?

Not always, but it's the most efficient time if they are sagging, leaking at seams or undersized. New drip edge goes on with the roof, and gutters hang right below it. See our gutters page.

Is metal roofing a good choice in Delaware County?

It can be, especially for long-term owners and outbuildings. It costs more up front than shingles. In Powell, switching materials triggers extra review, and HOAs may restrict it. Read more on our metal roofing page.

Can I install solar panels on my roof in an HOA neighborhood?

Often yes, with approval. Scioto Reserve's rules, for example, require Design Review Board approval and limit panels to non-street or rear elevations. If the roof is near the end of its life, replace it before the panels go on.

What happens if you find rotten decking during tear-off?

We photograph it, show you, and replace damaged sheets at the per-sheet price written in your contract. Rot is common around old leaks, chimneys and poorly ventilated eaves.

Does a roof inspection cost anything?

No. Our inspections are free anywhere in Delaware County and include photos of every slope. There is no obligation to hire us afterward.

Do you help with insurance claims after a storm?

Yes. We document damage, write the scope and meet your adjuster. We don't negotiate claims on your behalf or take power of attorney. See our storm damage roof repair page and Ohio roof insurance claims guide.

Which part of Delaware County do you cover?

All of it — from Powell, Lewis Center and Genoa Township in the south to Sunbury, Galena, Ashley and Ostrander. See every community on our service areas page.
