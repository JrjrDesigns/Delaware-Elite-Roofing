Housing stock

A stagecoach town that became a city

Sunbury was platted in 1816 with a town square set aside for a town hall, and a state historical marker for the 1868 Sunbury Town Hall remembers the community as an Ohio stagecoach town from 1820 to 1873. Growth along the I-71 and US-36/SR-37 corridor has changed the picture quickly: the population rose from 4,389 in 2010 to 6,614 in 2020, and Sunbury became a city on October 22, 2021. Tanger Outlets Columbus, at I-71 Exit 131, carries a Sunbury address.

Northstar, an 1,800-acre community with Sunbury addresses, sits in Berkshire and Kingston townships served by Big Walnut schools and built around the NorthStar Golf Club. Developed by Nationwide Realty Investors and The Robert Weiler Co., it brought in a long list of builders, including Del Webb, Fischer, M/I, Rockford, Schottenstein, P&D, Virginia, Manor and AMH. That variety matters when a roof needs work, because two houses on the same street may carry entirely different shingle lines. Our guide to builder-grade roof lifespan explains how production shingles age and what to watch for.

Storm exposure

The 2024 EF1 and the 2020 EF0 near Sunbury

On the night of March 14, 2024, an EF1 tornado with peak winds of 110 mph crossed Delaware County from near US-23 south of Delaware, through Berlin Township and past Sunbury and Galena on its way into Licking County. The Delaware Gazette listed the City of Sunbury among the affected jurisdictions, and Big Walnut schools closed the next day. Countywide, at least 254 homes and businesses were affected.

Four years earlier, on September 7, 2020, an EF0 with 80 mph winds ran from east of Delaware to about 4.5 miles northwest of Sunbury, crossing Alum Creek near North 3 B's & K Road. The National Weather Service noted one home with partial uplift of its roof and shingles scattered, along with 60–65 mph straight-line winds in the Delaware and Berkshire area. Many newer lots around Sunbury are open, so wind reaches roofs with little to slow it.

If you have not had your roof looked at since either storm, our storm damage roof repair team will inspect, photograph and document what we find, and our roof insurance claims guide covers Ohio's claim-handling deadlines. For active leaks, call (740) 555-0177 for emergency roof tarping.

On the job

We see a lot of this after high-wind nights around Sunbury — shingles that look fine from the driveway but have lost their seal. Wind lifts the tab, breaks the adhesive strip and lets it settle back down nearly flat. From the ground nothing looks wrong. Up close, you can lift the tab with a finger, and that unsealed shingle is the one that blows off in the next storm. We check seals by hand across every slope, not just where debris landed.

Permits

Roofing permits in Sunbury

Even though Sunbury is now a city, residential building permits for Sunbury are issued by Delaware County Building Safety, the same office that handles the townships and villages. The Residential Code of Ohio generally requires building-department approval for a re-roof unless it is a minor repair, and it limits roofs to two layers before a full tear-off is required. Sunbury also has its own zoning office with a residential application process, so we confirm with the city whether anything else applies to your property before work starts. Our Delaware County roof permits guide explains how the offices divide the work, and Northstar homeowners can lean on our HOA-approved roof replacement service for association submittals.

Storms tend to bring door-to-door roofers. Ohio's Home Solicitation Sales Act gives you until midnight of the third business day to cancel a contract signed at your home, and the Ohio Department of Insurance advises against signing a power of attorney over to a contractor or paying the full amount before the work is done. A reputable roofer will give you a written estimate, proof of insurance and time to think it over.

Frequently asked — Sunbury

Sunbury is a city now. Does it issue its own roofing permits?

Residential building permits for Sunbury come from Delaware County Building Safety. We also check with the city's zoning office so nothing is missed, and we handle the filing for you.

My roof looked fine after the 2024 tornado. Could it still be damaged?

Yes. High winds can break shingle seals without tearing shingles off, and unsealed tabs often fail in the next storm. A free roof inspection checks the seals by hand.

Can you match the shingles on my Northstar home for a repair?

Usually, but it takes identifying the exact product first, since many different builders worked in Northstar. We find the closest current match and show you before we install it.

If my Sunbury storm claim stalls, what timeline can I hold my insurer to?

Under OAC 3901-1-54, insurers must acknowledge a claim within 15 days and accept or deny it within 21 days of proof of loss, or explain the delay. Call (740) 555-0177 and we will help document your claim.

Do you work on the older homes around Sunbury's town square?

Yes. Older homes call for a repair-first approach, and we will tell you honestly whether flashing or chimney work can extend the life of your existing roof. See our roof leak repair page.
